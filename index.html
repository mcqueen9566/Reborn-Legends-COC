Clan Rules

⚔️ War Attacks

Use ALL your attacks in every war. Missing an attack without notice will result in being benched for the next war.

📦 Donations

Donate max troops whenever possible. We win together when we support each other. Keep those Clan Castle troops filled!

🏰 Clan Capital

Use all 6 of your Clan Capital attacks every weekend. Upgrade the districts and earn those raid medals!

📈 CWL Performance

During CWL, follow the war plan, hit your assigned targets, and communicate with the Co-Leaders. Every star counts.

🤝 Respect & Activity

Be active daily. Respect your clanmates. No drama. If you're going to be inactive, let a Co-Leader know.

🚫 No Rushing

We value quality over quantity. Upgrade your heroes and defenses before rushing to the next Town Hall.
